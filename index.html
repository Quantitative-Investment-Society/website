A research group for quantitative finance.

QIS is a student group at the University of Michigan. We study the mathematics, statistics, and software that underpin the field.

We were founded in 2018 by four computer science students, who went onto found their own hedge fund startup. Members today learn together through coursework, projects, and network with industry partners.

  1. 01

    Education

    Lectures and coursework in probability, statistics, and quantitative finance.

  2. 02

    Projects

    Applied work on data, models, and strategy research.

  3. 03

    Competitions

    Team entries in quant trading and research competitions.

  4. 04

    Networking

    Long-running relationships with firms and alumni.

How to join.

Everyone starts as a general member. From there, members can apply to become an Associate on one of our project tracks.

Step 1 · Open to all

General Member

Start here — everyone joins as a general member by filling out the application below.

  • Speaker sessions with quant firms
  • Technical workshops
  • Access to our Slack and resource library
Application →
Step 2 · Selective · application + interview

QR / QT / QDev Associate

Once you're a general member, apply and interview to join a cohort on one of three tracks: Quantitative Research, Quantitative Trading, or Quantitative Development.

  • Weekly meetings and education sessions
  • Team projects on your track
  • Enter trading competitions as a team
